Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb): a ~100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.

## The monologue

> Hero. Yes, the cape, the sword, the speech. Heard it. Third time this week. Only— ...Tuesday. Sit. Not the throne. The other chair. The one that doesn't wobble.
>
> You want the Ashen Crown? Take it. It pinches—eleven years now, since I pulled it off King Maro's head and learned crowns come with no instructions. Or lunch breaks.
>
> My ravens bring lists now. Lists! Grain levies, roof leaks, a dragon with a toothache. I conquered the coast for this?
>
> So. Swing your sword, hero. I'll hold still. Just— count to three first. Slowly. I want to hear counting when nobody's shouting.

Exactly 100 words, 577 characters. The tiredness lives in the rhythm — the stalled sentences, the lost thread, the small deflections — never in a plain statement that he is weary. The speaker is a specific villain: eleven years wearing the Ashen Crown he took from King Maro, now drowning in grain levies, roof leaks, and a dragon with a toothache.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only, no randomness, no network, no clock) writes the monologue deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output.
